# Topic Scoping

## Objective

Define the scope of a survey before literature collection.

## Required Questions

- What problem domain is covered?
- What is the time range?
- What are the major research branches?
- What are excluded topics?

## Output

A survey specification containing:

- Title
- Research scope
- Keywords
- Inclusion criteria
- Exclusion criteria
- Expected taxonomy

## Example

Topic: Blockchain-enabled Federated Learning

Scope:
- Robust aggregation
- Verification mechanisms
- Incentive mechanisms
- Privacy preservation
- Blockchain infrastructure
