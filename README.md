# Academic Survey Skill

An AI-assisted workflow for writing high-quality academic survey papers.

## Goal

This project extends the idea of latex-arxiv-SKILL from paper generation to systematic survey engineering. It targets computer science survey articles for journals such as Software Journal, Chinese Journal of Computers, Journal of Computer Research and Development, Acta Automatica Sinica, ACM Computing Surveys, and related venues.

## Workflow

1. Topic scoping
2. Literature collection
3. Literature organization
4. Taxonomy construction
5. Research evolution analysis
6. Method comparison
7. Challenges and future trends
8. Survey writing and reference verification

## Design Principles

- Build knowledge structure before writing
- Avoid paper-by-paper summaries
- Emphasize taxonomy, comparison and trend analysis
- Support Chinese and English academic writing styles
- Maintain reproducible literature management
